# Layered interpretation — mock for Siva's chart

Research mock only; nothing here is built. Values come from the app's own
engine (`calc-core.js`, `shadbala.js`) for Siva: 16 Jun 1970, 13:20 IST,
Chennai, Virgo ascendant, mean nodes.

Five layers, one question each. Each layer is shown on its own; there is no
blended score.

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| 1. Functional | What does the planet bring? |
| 2. Strength | How much, and how good? |
| 3. Association | Whose influence does it carry? |
| 4. Dasha | When? |
| 5. Transit | What triggers it now? |

  constructive, but the badhaka flag says delays or obstruction in those
  same matters. As a maraka, health of self or family deserves care.
- **Venus MD from Dec 2029:** the best functional benefic, balanced tone —
  the chart's strongest stretch of promise.
